Expedition — Florida Panhandle (July 6–10, 2025)

I planned and led a 5-day, self-designed exploration of the Florida Panhandle's coastal ecosystem and its economy: documenting marine biodiversity at Miramar Beach and the Pensacola Fishing Pier, studying the regional agricultural and maritime trade at farmers markets and HarborWalk Village, and examining the Navy's cultural footprint in Pensacola at the Blue Angels Air Show.

"The first was Personal Development... The second was the Expedition — a five-day trip I designed and led myself along the Florida Panhandle... It didn't go as planned — a thunderstorm canceled a snorkeling session, a closed road forced a last-minute reroute, my gear malfunctioned mid-dive — but adjusting on the spot was part of what made it exhilarating rather than stressful."
